Check readiness:

Every binary exposes /healthz, /readyz and /metrics on its admin port. Liveness
is "the process is scheduling goroutines"; readiness is "dependencies are
reachable and start-up finished". A failing /readyz is a pod out of the load
balancer, never a restart:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "usslp.fullname" . }}-label-service 9090:9090
  curl -s localhost:9090/readyz | jq
